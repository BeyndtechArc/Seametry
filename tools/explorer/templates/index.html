A tokenized stock is not an ordinary token

Its issuer can freeze it where it sits, pause all movement of it, take it back from any wallet, decide who may receive it, and change how many of them you appear to hold. All of that is written on chain for anyone who decodes it, and almost nobody decodes it.

A basket of eight tokenized stocks is a basket of eight instruments that eight other parties still control. Every bundler shipping today assumes the tokens inside behave like tokens. For tokenized equities that assumption is false, and it fails in ways that stay invisible until the day they matter.

A bundle is not an ETF

A bundle buys you several tokens in one transaction, and you end up holding the tokens. An ETF is a mechanism: shares exist because someone deposited the basket to create them, and can always be destroyed to reclaim it. That loop is what keeps a share's price honest.

Traditionally the loop is restricted to authorized participants, a closed list of large banks, and that gatekeeping is much of why retail pays a spread. On Solana it can be open to every wallet. That is the product.

What this does not establish

  • One issuer only. Backpack Securities is not covered, and its dividend mechanism is still an open question.
  • A moment, not a trend. Figures come from one slot, and the share moves as issuers update fields.
  • Nothing about intent. An issuer holding a power is not an issuer misusing one, and every issuer holding a given power gets the identical sentence here.
  • Nothing about depth. Whether any of these can be traded at size is a separate measurement, and a separate ceiling.
