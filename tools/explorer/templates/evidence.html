What this does not establish

  • One issuer. xStocks only. Backpack Securities is not covered, and whether its dividends arrive as a multiplier change or as newly minted tokens is unsettled. A fixture capture settles it, not a documentation page.
  • A moment, not a trend. One slot. The staleness share moves as issuers update fields, and the large splits will eventually be cleaned up.
  • Nothing about intent. An issuer holding a power is not an issuer misusing one.
  • Nothing about depth. Whether any of these can be traded at size is a separate measurement.
