Question:    Which published Seametry basket do I want to inspect, and can I buy it whole from its record?
Sections:    Page header (which room am I in, on which network?); Filter bar (which baskets do I want in front of me?); ruled basket register (which basket, how much of it is admitted?); basket record with its purchase workbench (how do I buy this composition in one decision?).
Components:  App shell (Baskets and Build a basket are routes in the Allocation group, drawn as shell tabs); PageHeader; Filter bar (the shared FilterBar, Theme and Access groups); Basket register; Basket plate; Lot mark stack; RouteAction for the in-record jump to the purchase; Key inside the existing AllocationFlow.
Data:        Published basket metadata from lib/allocation/indexes.ts; admission per constituent from allocationOffer(); the plate sets one rosette per constituent, filled when the current snapshot admits it and an empty socket when the engine refused it or never assessed it; its turn and engraving come from the basket code. The record names each refused constituent's blocking fact from allocationOffer().refused and each never-assessed one as such. The 12 October 2026 capture (depth-2026-10-12.md) assayed every constituent that had never been assessed: ten admitted with WARN, ARM and IREN refused on execution impact, VCXx refused on depth; OPAIPon has no listing evidence to capture from. AI Cloud then took AVGOx and MU, both admitted, in place of ARM and IREN (Storm, 12 October 2026), so every published basket except the research-only Private Frontier can be bought whole.
States:      default shows all ten rows; searching and filtered narrow the view and the count line says "Showing n of m"; empty says nothing matches and to widen search or filters; a partially admitted basket shows empty sockets on its plate and "n of m admitted", has no purchase as published, and offers a remix from what is admitted; research-only shows its sockets empty where the snapshot does not admit and "Research only"; loading and error are the route's own loading boundary and error page, since the register is server-rendered from a committed snapshot.
Ceremony:    None while browsing. The constituent settlement register inside the purchase is the only motion, and only while real wallet or network work runs.
Names:       Baskets (navigation and the published register); Build a basket (the custom route); Allocation (what a holder owns after purchase); Admission, Access, Holds (row labels); Buy basket (the in-record jump and the Key on a published basket); Buy allocation (the Key on a custom build).
Assumptions: Storm, 12 October 2026: a generated engraved plate in the Fortune Five artwork's manner replaces the per-basket emblems, so no basket needs commissioned artwork (a weight ring was tried first and dropped: its ten instances read alike; using the Fortune Five picture itself was rejected because an Alloy's artwork belongs to that Alloy and would make all ten rows identical); the pitch-deck slides (clients/web/public/Slide-*.png) set the dark touchstone's hue, an olive-black, and are not basket assets. The custom section switch, custom filter popover and mobile overflow menu are removed because the App shell and Filter bar contracts already own those jobs. The mobile central plus keeps opening Baskets, the Allocation entry, and the register closes with a Text action to Build a basket; a Quiet link under the title was tried and removed (Storm, 12 October 2026: it read as a stray control on a phone). Reshuffling the bottom tabs instead would leave Sponsor unreachable on phones. On a phone the row drops Holds and Access, since the plate shows the composition.
