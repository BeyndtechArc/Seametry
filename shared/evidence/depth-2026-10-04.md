Shortfall is how much worse the realised rate at that size is than the rate
at the smallest size that priced, in basis points rounded up. It is computed
in exact arithmetic from the two quotes, not taken from the aggregator's own
price impact field. That field is a fraction rather than a percent, which was
measured against realised rates rather than assumed, and it is absent at very
small sizes, which is not the same as zero.

## What this does not establish

- **One aggregator, one moment.** Depth moves continuously and routes change
  between calls. These are three samples taken seconds apart.
- **Executable is not the same as executed.** A quote is an expiring
  proposition. Nothing here was signed or submitted.
- **Buying only.** Depth selling into USDC is a separate measurement and
  can differ sharply on a thin pool.
- **Nothing about intent.** A shallow pool says nothing about an issuer.
