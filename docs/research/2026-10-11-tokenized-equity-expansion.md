# Tokenized-equity expansion watch

**Observed:** 11 October 2026.  
**Purpose:** admission backlog and Basket research. Nothing in this file is admitted merely because an issuer announced it.

The tokenized-equity universe is moving faster than Seametry's current admissions snapshot. The Basket register may surface these themes as research, but a constituent becomes executable only after its mint, legal Grade, Token-2022 controls, issuer terms and executable depth have been captured and evaluated under the current Good Delivery policy.

## Securitize Stocks

Securitize announced its Solana tokenized-stock product on 8 October 2026. The initial set includes AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, GOOG, TSLA, META, AMZN, NFLX, CRCL, SPCX, MSTR and PLTR security entitlements.

Source:
- https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Securitize-Launches-Global-Onchain-Trading-of-U-S--Stocks-with-Security-Entitlements/default.aspx

This creates another cross-issuer comparison opportunity, but the product must not infer equivalence with Backpack or xStocks. Capture the legal and token mechanics first.

## Ondo Intelligent Portfolios

Ondo launched professionally designed single-token portfolios on 24 September 2026.

Source:
- https://ondo.finance/blog/introducing-ondo-intelligent-portfolios

This is useful competitive evidence for paid curation, methodology and portfolio discovery. It is not the same ownership model as a Seametry Allocation: an Allocation leaves the constituent tokens in the holder's wallet, while an Ondo Intelligent Portfolio is itself one tokenized portfolio product.

## Private markets

Ondo Private Markets launched in October 2026 with tokenized notes whose payout references private-company shares. The ticker convention ends in `Pon`; OPAIPon is the OpenAI Private Market Note.

Sources:
- https://ondo.finance/private-markets
- https://app.ondo.finance/private-markets/opaipon

xStocks and Fundrise announced VCXx, a tokenized representation of the Fundrise Innovation Fund, whose portfolio includes late-stage private technology exposure.

Source:
- https://xstocks.com/news/xstocks-and-fundrise-partner-to-tokenize-vcx-fund-unlocking-onchain-exposure-to-leading-private-tech-companies

Backpack's SPCX is a different shape again: Backpack describes a security entitlement connected to SpaceX shares.

Source:
- https://learn.backpack.exchange/blog/tokenized-spacex-spcx

These belong beside one another in research because the legal shapes differ. They must not be presented as interchangeable "pre-IPO stocks".
