# The ETF spine, and what it displaced

**Decision record. Historical, append only, not a living document.**
Recorded 23 September 2026.

The living authority is `../PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE.md`. Read this for why, never
for what is currently true.

---

## Context

Two product shapes were documented side by side and neither retired the other:

- The `seametry-0x` doctrine (22 September): a basket broker whose baskets live
  in a keyless Hall program, mobile first.
- A business architecture recovered from a lost session (15 September lineage):
  an instrument intelligence and execution evidence platform, Terminal first,
  containing no basket content at all.

A session earlier on 23 September resolved this in favour of the Terminal, with
baskets deferred to phase 3. That resolution was made without knowledge of the
validation below and is superseded by this record.

## What happened

At a Superteam pitch clinic ahead of Stocklana, position intelligence was
pitched to a room including the Superteam lead and NectarFi's founder. The
response: position intelligence is not a large enough market on its own, ETFs
on Solana are the more interesting problem, and a better UX stock broker
competes directly with an already backed team. The Superteam lead appeared
interested in the ETF direction as well.

Full record, including how the advice was weighed and what independent evidence
supports it, is in `../PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE.md` section 11.

## Decisions

**D1. Baskets are the spine. Intelligence is the admission standard.**
The Hall moves from deferred to central. The assay (grade, prerogatives, live
multiplier, depth at size) is reframed from the product to what makes a
constituent admissible and a basket defensible. It remains independently
sellable as the API surface.

Reverses the earlier same day decision that made the Terminal the spine.

**D2. The wedge is the open authorized participant set.**
Creation and redemption restricted to a closed list of banks is the traditional
arrangement. On chain it can be open to every wallet. This is what separates the
product from a bundler, and it is why the program can never hold a key and can
never take a price as an input.

**D3. The Terminal is re scoped to a creation and redemption console.**
Not a trading terminal, not a broker. Its job is the authorized participant's
job: NAV against share price, depth at size per constituent, assembly cost
against melt proceeds.

**D4. Mobile is bounded away from consumer brokerage,** deliberately, to avoid a
contested lane held by a funded team with distribution. Mobile is a holding and
approval surface.

**D5. Phasing is reordered around which gates are real.**
Allocations carry real mainnet usage early because nothing is pooled and no
claim is issued, so no legal gate applies. The Hall proves its thesis on devnet
at zero legal risk. Only the mainnet Hall waits on a legal read and an audit.

**D6. Lending constituents is excluded from v1.**
Inside the Hall it breaks the redemption guarantee, breaks the balance
invariant (an outgoing loan is indistinguishable from an issuer seizure to
`sync`), and breaks the argument that no instruction can move a holder's
assets. At the allocation layer it is defensible but unvalidated: borrow demand
for tokenized equities is unevidenced, and it adds a securities lending
regulatory profile on top of an already unsettled question.

**D7. One process, not three.**
`SERVICE_CATALOG.md` specified three processes while its own extraction rule
required a measured criterion before splitting. The rule wins. Service
boundaries stay enforced in package structure so a later split is
configuration, not a rewrite. The first split will be Execution, when real
mainnet execution exists.

**D8. Retention becomes versioned policy with three tiers.**
`ENGINEERING_STANDARD.md` required raw evidence retention with no expiry, which
is unaffordable at any scale. Hot, warm, and cold tiers, with the rule that
evidence referenced by a decision, approval, or receipt is never pruned, and
that a pruned range reports as pruned rather than as unavailable.

**D9. Infrastructure targets free tiers, and storage is the cost driver.**
Egress is not the constraint for this workload; retained evidence volume is.
Local Docker for development. First deploy targets a free tier with enough
block storage to hold retained evidence, rather than a managed database with a
monthly floor. Revisit when there is revenue.

## What this does not settle

Whether an open authorized participant set is worth paying for. Whether share
pools can be seeded deeply enough for a one swap retail path to exist. Whether
the phase 3 legal question resolves favourably.
